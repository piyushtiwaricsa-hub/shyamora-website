Frequently asked questions

Good to know before we talk.

Which countries do you support?
SHYAMORA is positioned to work with businesses serving the USA, UK, UAE, and Canada. Specific coverage, working hours, channels, and service requirements are confirmed during discovery.
What e-commerce services can I enquire about?
You can ask about customer support, order management, returns and refunds workflows, NDR/RTO coordination, and related marketplace operations. Final availability and scope depend on the requirements discussed.
How does pricing work?
Quotes are provided in USD. The fee depends on the agreed scope, workload, volumes, coverage, channels, and tools. You receive the applicable scope and commercial terms before work starts.
Can you guarantee sales, delivery, or marketplace outcomes?
No. Sales, marketplace decisions, rankings, carrier performance, delivery outcomes, and customer behavior are outside SHYAMORA's control. We can discuss the operational tasks and service scope that are within an agreed engagement.
How do we get started?
Book a free initial consultation using the enquiry form. Share only general information about your needs; do not submit passwords, payment details, or sensitive customer data.
Is the consultation a commitment?
No. An enquiry or initial consultation does not create a service agreement. Any engagement requires a separate written agreement on scope, pricing, and terms.

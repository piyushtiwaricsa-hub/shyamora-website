Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Have a question that is not covered here? Send us a message and tell us what you need.

Which marketplaces can you support?
We can discuss seller support related to Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, Shopify, Myntra, AJIO and Snapdeal. The specific work depends on your account, category, marketplace requirements and agreed scope.
Can you guarantee sales, ranking or account approval?
No. Marketplace decisions, search placement, sales and approvals depend on the platform and many factors outside any service provider's control. We focus on the agreed support tasks and do not promise outcomes.
What do you need to get started?
Usually, a description of your products, the marketplace(s) you use, your current priorities and any relevant listing or account information. Never send passwords or sensitive identity documents over an unsecured channel; we can discuss a suitable way to share information.
How is pricing decided?
Pricing depends on the agreed scope, volume, marketplace and whether support is a one-time project or ongoing. The package amounts shown above are editable placeholders, not published quotes.
Do you work with new sellers and existing stores?
You can enquire whether your setup, catalog or operations needs fit the available support. We will clarify the deliverables and any dependencies before confirming work.

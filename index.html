Frequently asked questions

Good to know before we talk.

What services does SHYAMORA provide?
Customer support, order management, NDR and RTO follow-ups, returns and exchange coordination, courier and marketplace coordination with authorized access, and operational reporting—based on the agreed scope.
Which businesses can work with SHYAMORA?
We welcome enquiries from e-commerce and D2C businesses, including fashion, beauty, electronics, home, health and wellness brands. We will discuss whether the requested workflow is a fit.
Can I outsource only NDR and RTO management?
Yes, you can enquire about a focused NDR or RTO scope. Responsibilities, volume, customer communication and reporting will be confirmed before a quotation.
How does onboarding work?
We learn about your workflows, agree on scope and escalation paths, and identify any access or information needed. Work starts after responsibilities and authorization are clear.
How is pricing calculated?
Published monthly figures are starting reference prices, not unlimited commitments. Final pricing depends on order and ticket volume, working hours, channels, requested services and agreed scope. Taxes extra where applicable.
Do you provide customized plans?
Yes. Custom and enterprise scopes can be discussed based on your requirements.
What access or information is needed?
This depends on the agreed work. Access should be limited to what is necessary, provided only with client authorization, and handled using mutually agreed security practices. Do not send passwords or sensitive personal data through this enquiry form.
What are your working hours?
Working hours and coverage are agreed with each client as part of the scope. Please include your preferred coverage in your enquiry.

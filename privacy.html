Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This page describes the limited information handled when you use this static website to prepare an enquiry. Please review and update it if your actual business processes differ.

Information you choose to provide

The enquiry form asks for your name and message, and optionally a phone number, marketplace and type of support. The website itself does not submit form entries to a server or store them in a website database. On submission, it prepares a message and opens WhatsApp so you can review it and choose whether to send it. If you send the message, WhatsApp processes it under its own privacy terms.

Contacting us directly

If you email SHYAMORA or contact us through WhatsApp, the information in that communication is received through the relevant provider and may be used to respond to your enquiry and discuss requested services. Contact: shyamorasellersolutions@gmail.com.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not intentionally use advertising cookies, analytics scripts or paid tracking services. GitHub Pages may process technical request data to deliver the site under its own terms and policies.

Data minimization and security

Please do not send passwords, payment card information or sensitive identity documents through this website or an unsecured message. Share only information needed to discuss your enquiry. No online transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

External services and links

Links to WhatsApp and email open services operated by third parties. Their privacy practices are governed by their own notices; review those before sharing information.

Your questions

For a question or request about information you have sent to SHYAMORA, contact shyamorasellersolutions@gmail.com.
